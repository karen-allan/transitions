ADVANCE DIRECTIVE

Made under the Health Care (Consent) and Care Facility (Admission) Act

The use of this form is voluntary. Before completing this Advance Directive, it is advisable to obtain legal advice and the advice of a health care provider about the possible implications of this Advance Directive, and your choices about the types of health care for which you might give or refuse consent under this Advance Directive. The questions posed here may not be all encompassing, as you may need more information than is provided here.

    Note 2 - Information for Witnesses

    (a) The following persons may not be a witness:
  • i. A person who provides personal care, health care or financial services to the adult for compensation, other than a lawyer or a notary public;
  • ii. A spouse, child, parent, employee or agent of a person described in paragraph (a);
  • iii. A person who is under 19 years of age
  • iv. A person who does not understand the type of communication used by the Adult, unless the person receives interpretive assistance to understand that type of communication.


    • (b) Only one witness is required if the witness is a lawyer or notary public.



    (c) You should not witness the Advance Directive if you have reason to believe that:
  • i. The Adult is incapable of making, changing, or revoking an Advance Directive;
  • ii. Fraud, undue pressue or some other form of abuse or neglect was used to induce the Adult to make the Advance Directive or to change or revoke a previous Advance Directive.
