WITNESSES TO ADULT’S SIGNATURE:
The following persons may not be a witness:
  • A person who provides personal, health or financial services, other than a lawyer or a notary public;
  • Your spouse, child, parent, employee or agent.
  • A person who is under 19 years of age.
  • A person who does not understand your method of communication.
You should not witness the Advance Directive if you believe that:
  • The Adult is incapable of making, changing, or revoking an Advance Directive;
  • Fraud or undue pressure was used to induce them to change or revoke their Advance Directive.
