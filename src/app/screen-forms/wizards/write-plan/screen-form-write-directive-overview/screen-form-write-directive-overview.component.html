WITNESSES TO ADULT’S SIGNATURE:
The following persons may not be a witness:
  • A person who provides personal care, health care or financial services to the adult for compensation, other than a lawyer or a notary public;
  • A spouse, child, parent, employee or agent of a person described in paragraph (a);
  • A person who is under 19 years of age
  • A person who does not understand the communication used by the adult unless the person receives interpretive assistance.
You should not witness the Advance Directive if you have reason to believe that:
  • The Adult is incapable of making, changing, or revoking an Advance Directive;
  • Fraud, undue pressure or some form of abuse or neglect was used to induce the Adult to change or revoke the Advance Directive.
