SECTION 3: CONDITIONS FOR HEALTH CARE:

3.0. If I am sedated and unable to communicate, I would like the sedation lifted so that I can rationally consider my situation and decide for myself to accept or refuse a particular therapy:

  • 3.0a. Yes, lift my sedation.
  • 3.0b. No, do not lift my sedation.

3.1. I direct that I receive only such care as will keep me comfortable and pain free, and that my dying not be prolonged should any of the following circumstances occur:

  • 3.1a. An acute life-threatening illness of an irreversible nature.
  • 3.1b. Chronic debilitating suffering of a permanent nature.
  • 3.1c. Advanced dementia.

3.2. I direct that I receive only such care as will keep me comfortable and pain free, and that my dying not be prolonged should any of the following circumstances occur:

  • 3.2a. Electrical, mechanical or other artificial stimulation of my heart.
  • 3.2b. Respirator or ventilator.
  • 3.2c. Artificial feeding e.g. G tube, NG tube, or central intravenous line.
  • 3.2d. Being fed should I no longer be able to feed myself.
  • 3.2e. Artificial hydration by intravenous line.
  • 3.2f. Antibiotics.
  • 3.2g. Transfer to an intensive care unit or similar facility.

3.1. In this Advance Directive, I specifically direct the following regarding palliative care:

  • 3a. Provide necessary medication to control my pain and symptoms even if such medication might shorten my life.
  • 3b. Provide me with palliative care.
  • 3.3c. I would like to die in my home.
  • 3.3c. I would like to die in a hospice.
